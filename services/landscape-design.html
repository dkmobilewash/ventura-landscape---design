Overview

Design That Starts With How You Want to Live Outside

Great landscaping doesn't start with plants — it starts with a plan. Custom landscape design is the blueprint for everything that follows: where you'll gather, how water moves through the yard, what blooms in spring, and how the whole space looks from your kitchen window. A good design saves you money by getting it right the first time, instead of planting now and ripping it out in two years.

In Ventura County, smart design also means designing for our Mediterranean climate. Long dry summers, mild winters, ocean influence near the coast, and real water-conservation requirements all shape what thrives here. We lean on California native plants and drought-tolerant species that look lush without drinking through your water budget — paired with the right irrigation and materials to match.

What sets Landscaping Pros Of Ventura apart is that we don't just hand you a pretty drawing. We're the team that builds it, too. That means the design is realistic, buildable, and tailored to your budget — no surprises between the plan and the finished yard.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

🌿
How much does landscape design cost?

It depends on the size and complexity of your property and whether you want a full plan or a focused concept for one area. We offer a free initial consultation so we can scope your project and give you a clear, honest number before any work begins.

How long does the design process take?

Most residential designs take a couple of weeks from the first site walk to a finished plan, depending on revisions and the scope. We keep you in the loop the whole way and never rush the part that everything else depends on.

Can I make changes to the design?

Absolutely — revisions are a normal, expected part of the process. We want you to love the plan before a single shovel hits the ground, so we refine the layout, plant choices, and materials with you until it's right.

Do you specialize in drought-tolerant designs?

Yes. Drought-tolerant and California native landscaping is core to what we do. We design beautiful, water-wise yards that look full and vibrant while keeping water use — and your bill — low, in line with local conservation requirements.

Do you also install the landscape you design?

We do. We're a full design-build company, so the same team that designs your landscape can handle hardscaping, planting, irrigation, lighting, and ongoing maintenance — one point of contact from start to finish.
