Overview

The Backyard Becomes the Best Room in the House

An outdoor kitchen turns "we should grill sometime" into the place everyone gathers. Instead of running back and forth to the indoor kitchen, you've got a built-in grill, prep counters, storage, and a spot for guests to pull up a stool and keep you company while you cook. It's the difference between hosting and actually enjoying your own party.

Few places are better suited to outdoor cooking than Ventura County. Our long, dry summers and mild coastal evenings mean a BBQ island earns its keep nearly every month of the year — Sunday tri-tip, weeknight dinners, game-day spreads, and late nights by the fire. A well-built outdoor kitchen isn't a seasonal splurge here; it's an everyday upgrade to how you live.

What sets Ventura Landscape & Design apart is that we build outdoor kitchens as part of the whole outdoor space. We coordinate the island with your patio, seating, lighting, and gas lines so everything works together and the finished result looks like it was always meant to be there — not like a grill cart parked on the concrete.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

🌿
What materials are best for an outdoor kitchen?

It comes down to look and durability. Stucco islands match many Ventura County homes and are budget-friendly; natural stone and tile veneers offer a premium finish; and for countertops, sealed granite and porcelain hold up beautifully to sun and heat. We'll walk you through the trade-offs and match materials to your home's style.

How much does an outdoor kitchen cost?

It ranges widely depending on size, appliances, and finishes — a simple built-in grill island is very different from a full kitchen with a bar, sink, fridge, and stone veneer. We provide a free, detailed estimate so you know exactly what your project involves before you commit.

Should I go with natural gas or propane?

Both work well. Natural gas means you never run out mid-cookout, but it requires running a gas line to the island. Propane is more flexible for placement and avoids that plumbing. We'll help you weigh the layout and your existing utilities to pick the right setup.

Do I need a permit for an outdoor kitchen?

Often, yes — especially when gas, electrical, or plumbing is involved. As a licensed company, we know the local requirements and handle the permitting so your kitchen is built safely and inspected properly.

Will an HOA approve an outdoor kitchen?

Most HOAs allow them, though some have guidelines on height, placement, and finishes. We're familiar with HOA review in Ventura County communities and can design the island to fit those rules and help with the approval submission.
