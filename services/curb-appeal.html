Overview

First Impressions Happen at the Curb

Everyone decides something about your home before they reach the front door. Buyers form an opinion from the car. Guests size it up walking from the driveway. Even you feel it every time you come home. A front yard that's overgrown, faded, or just tired drags the whole property down — and the fix is usually simpler and more affordable than people expect.

Curb appeal work is about the high-impact details that read instantly: crisply edged planting beds, a fresh layer of bark or mulch, healthy color near the entry, trimmed and shaped shrubs, and a clean, welcoming path to the door. We pull out what's overgrown or half-dead, define the beds, and replant with low-water, Southern California-appropriate plants that look full now and keep looking good through our dry summers.

At Ventura Landscape & Design, we tailor the refresh to your goal. If you're getting ready to list, we make the yard photo-ready and help it show well for buyers. If you're staying put, we build a front yard you're proud to pull up to — designed around low-water planting so it stays sharp without becoming a chore or running up your water bill.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

🌿
How much does a curb appeal refresh cost?

It scales with the size of the yard and how much you want done — a focused entry-and-bed refresh is very different from a full front-yard makeover with new planting and bark throughout. Because curb appeal work targets high-impact, visible changes, it's often one of the more affordable projects we do. We'll give you a clear free estimate after seeing the yard.

Will this actually raise my home's value?

Curb appeal is consistently one of the best returns in real estate. A clean, attractive front yard shapes how buyers feel before they ever walk inside, helps your listing photos stand out, and signals a home that's been maintained. It won't replace a kitchen remodel, but dollar for dollar, few exterior improvements pay off as reliably.

How long does a refresh take?

Most front-yard refreshes are quick — often just a day or a few, depending on scope. Clean-ups and replanting move fast; larger makeovers that involve new beds or more planting take a little longer. If you're racing a listing date, let us know and we'll plan around it.

What's typically included?

A refresh usually covers removing overgrowth and dead material, weeding, defining and edging beds, trimming and shaping shrubs, fresh planting with drought-tolerant color, new bark or mulch, and tidying the entry and walkway. We build the package around your yard and your goals — nothing you don't need.

Can you keep it low-water?

Absolutely — and we recommend it. We lean on drought-tolerant plants and groundcovers that look full and colorful but thrive in our dry Southern California summers. You get a front yard that stays sharp with less watering, lower bills, and less ongoing upkeep.
