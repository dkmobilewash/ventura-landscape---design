Overview

A Maintained Yard, Without the Weekend Work

A well-kept yard doesn't happen on its own. Grass grows fast in spring, weeds creep in after every warm spell, and shrubs need shaping before they sprawl. Our lawn and yard maintenance service takes all of it off your plate with a consistent schedule, so your property always looks cared for — not like it's been put off another week.

Ventura County's Mediterranean climate keeps things green for much of the year, but it also means long, dry summers and real water-conservation rules to work around. We adjust mowing heights, watering checks, and feeding schedules to the seasons here — keeping turf healthy through coastal fog and inland heat without wasting a drop. For drought-tolerant and native plantings, that means knowing exactly when to prune and when to leave them be.

We work with homeowners who want their weekends back, property managers who need a dependable crew, and HOAs that expect common areas to look consistent every visit. Whatever the property, you get one local team that knows your yard and treats it like their own.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

🌿
How often should my lawn be serviced?

Most Ventura County yards do best on weekly service during the active growing season and can shift to bi-weekly through the cooler, slower months. We'll recommend a frequency based on your grass type, plantings, and how fast things grow on your property — and adjust it as the seasons change.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No long lock-in required. We offer straightforward ongoing service that you can adjust or pause as your needs change. Many homeowners and HOAs prefer a steady recurring schedule for the consistency, but we keep the terms simple and fair.

What's included in a standard visit?

A typical visit covers mowing, edging along walks and beds, line trimming, blowing off hard surfaces, and pulling or treating weeds. Shrub trimming, fertilization, and seasonal cleanups are folded in on the right schedule rather than every single visit.

Do you handle seasonal cleanups and fertilization?

Yes. Beyond routine mowing, we time fertilization, weed treatments, deeper pruning, and seasonal cleanups to the calendar so your lawn and plantings stay healthy year-round. It's all coordinated by the same crew that handles your regular visits.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing is based on the size of your property, the scope of work, and your service frequency — so a small residential lawn and a large HOA common area are quoted differently. We walk the property first and give you a clear, flat price per visit with no surprises.
