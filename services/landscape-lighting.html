Overview

Your Landscape Deserves a Second Act at Night

You spent good money on your trees, your patio, and your front entry — but for half of every day, no one can see any of it. Landscape lighting changes that. With the right fixtures in the right places, your home takes on a whole new character after sunset: warm pools of light along the walkway, gentle uplighting that turns a mature oak into a sculpture, and a glow that makes the whole property feel welcoming and cared for.

In Ventura County, our mild evenings practically beg you to stay outside. A well-lit yard extends the hours you actually use your patio, deck, and garden — whether you're hosting friends on a summer night or just enjoying a quiet glass of wine after work. Good lighting also pulls double duty: it lights stairs and pathways so no one stumbles, and it discourages anyone from poking around a dark side yard.

What sets Ventura Landscape & Design apart is that we treat lighting as design, not just wiring. We use professional-grade, low-voltage LED systems that sip power, last for years, and let us layer light with intention. And because we handle your whole landscape, the lighting is planned alongside the plants and hardscape — not bolted on as an afterthought.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

🌿
Is LED better than halogen for landscape lighting?

For nearly every yard, yes. LED fixtures use roughly 80% less energy, run cool, and last many years longer than halogen — which means lower bills and far fewer bulb changes. The light quality is excellent, and we can dial in warm tones that feel inviting rather than harsh.

How much does landscape lighting cost?

It depends on the size of your property and how many fixtures you want. A focused entry-and-path package is very different from lighting an entire estate. We provide a free estimate so you get a clear number, and many homeowners start with a core scheme and add to it over time.

Can the lights turn on and off automatically?

Absolutely. We set systems up on timers, dusk-to-dawn photocells, or smart controls you run from your phone — so your lighting comes on at sunset and shuts off on schedule without you thinking about it.

Will the wiring be visible in my yard?

No. Low-voltage lighting wire is buried in shallow trenches and tucked along beds and hardscape edges. Once we're finished, you see the light and the fixtures — not the wiring.

Do you light existing landscapes or only new ones?

Both. We add lighting to established yards all the time, and we also build it into new landscape and hardscape projects from the start. Either way, we design around what's already there to show your property at its best.
