Overview

An Instant, Established Lawn — Done Right the First Time

Sod is the fastest way to go from bare dirt or a tired, patchy yard to a thick green lawn. Instead of babying seed for months and hoping it fills in evenly, you get a mature lawn rolled out in a day. But the result only lasts if the groundwork is done properly — and that's where most DIY sod jobs fall apart. The soil prep, grading, and first weeks of watering matter just as much as the sod itself.

In Ventura County, the right grass choice is everything. Our Mediterranean climate brings long dry summers, mild winters, and coastal influence near the water that shifts to real heat inland. A cool-season Fescue stays green and soft underfoot, while warm-season Bermuda, St. Augustine, and Zoysia handle heat and sun with less water. We help you pick a variety that matches your sun exposure, foot traffic, and water budget — so your new lawn thrives instead of struggling against the climate.

We're also straight with homeowners about water. A new sod lawn drinks heavily for the first few weeks to root in, then settles into a much lower routine. We pair the right variety with efficient irrigation and a clear watering schedule so you establish the lawn quickly and stay within local conservation guidelines once it's rooted.

How It Works

Our Sod Installation Process

🌿

Site Assessment & Sod Selection

We measure your yard, check sun and soil, and help you choose the best variety — Fescue, Bermuda, St. Augustine, or Zoysia — for your space and water goals.

Soil Prep & Grading

We clear the old lawn or debris, amend and till the soil, and grade for smooth, even drainage away from your home — the foundation a lasting lawn depends on.

Precision Installation

Fresh-cut sod is laid in tight, staggered rows with clean, seamless seams and rolled for solid soil contact so it roots in fast and looks uniform.

Watering Plan & Follow-Up

We give you a clear watering schedule for the critical rooting period and check that your irrigation has the new lawn covered so it establishes strong.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

🌿
What's the best sod for Ventura County?

It depends on your yard. A Tall Fescue stays green year-round and feels soft underfoot, making it a popular all-around choice. For hot, sunny inland spots with less water to spare, warm-season Bermuda or Zoysia hold up beautifully, while St. Augustine handles partial shade well. We'll recommend the right variety after seeing your sun, soil, and how you use the space.

How much does sod installation cost per square foot?

Cost depends on the sod variety, your yard's size, and how much soil prep and grading the site needs — a smooth, ready-to-go yard costs less than one that needs an old lawn removed and regraded. We measure the area and give you a clear, itemized estimate up front so there are no surprises.

How long before I can walk on or use my new lawn?

Stay off the fresh sod for about two weeks while the roots take hold. Light foot traffic is usually fine after that, and the lawn is typically ready for normal use — kids, pets, and gatherings — in roughly three to four weeks, once it's firmly rooted into the soil below.

How much watering does new sod need?

New sod needs frequent, light watering for the first couple of weeks to root in — often a few short cycles a day in warm weather. After it establishes, you taper down significantly to a normal, efficient schedule. We give you a clear watering plan and make sure your irrigation covers the whole lawn evenly.

Can you remove my old lawn before installing sod?

Yes. Full prep is part of what we do — we remove the existing lawn or weeds, amend and till the soil, and grade the area before any sod goes down. Proper removal and prep are what keep the new lawn from inheriting old problems, so it's never a step we skip.
