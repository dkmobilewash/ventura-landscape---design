Overview

A Lawn That Stays Green Without the Upkeep

Artificial turf has come a long way from the stiff, plastic carpet you might remember. Today's premium synthetic grass uses multiple blade colors, varied blade heights, and a natural-looking thatch layer so it reads as a healthy lawn from the curb and feels soft underfoot. The difference is everything you don't do: no mowing, no fertilizing, no reseeding bare patches, and no dragging a hose around every evening to keep it alive through summer.

For Ventura County homeowners, the math is hard to argue with. California's long-running drought and steadily climbing water bills make a real lawn an expensive thing to keep green from June through October. Outdoor watering is the single biggest use of household water here, and turf eliminates that line item entirely. Many homeowners also qualify for turf-replacement and lawn-conversion rebates that help offset the install cost, and synthetic grass stays compliant no matter what watering restrictions are in place.

Beyond saving water, turf solves the everyday headaches a real lawn creates. It's ideal for pet areas — pet-rated turf drains quickly and rinses clean — and it gives kids a soft, even, mud-free surface to play on. From shady side yards where grass refuses to grow to sun-baked backyards and rooftop decks, synthetic turf delivers a consistent green that a living lawn simply can't in our climate.

How It Works

Our Artificial Turf Process

🌿

Consultation & Turf Selection

We measure the area, talk through how you'll use it, and help you pick the right turf — pet-rated, play-grade, or landscape — and blade style.

Removal & Base Prep

We strip out the old lawn, grade for drainage, and build a compacted aggregate base so the turf sits flat, drains fast, and never ripples.

Turf Installation

We roll out, seam, and secure the turf, keeping the grain consistent so there are no visible joints, then trim it cleanly to every edge.

Infill & Finishing

We brush in the right infill for stability and a natural feel, power-broom the blades upright, and walk the finished lawn with you.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

🌿
How much does artificial turf cost per square foot?

Installed cost depends on the turf grade you choose, the size of the area, and how much old lawn removal and base prep the site needs. There's more up-front cost than seeding a lawn, but with no water bill, no mowing, and a long lifespan it typically pays for itself over time. We'll measure your yard and give you a clear, itemized quote for free.

How long does artificial turf last?

A quality turf that's professionally installed commonly lasts 15 to 20 years or more, and most premium products carry a manufacturer warranty in that range. The base prep matters as much as the turf itself — a properly compacted, well-drained foundation is what keeps it from rippling or wearing unevenly over the years.

Does artificial turf get hot in the sun?

Turf does warm up in direct summer sun like any surface, but it cools quickly in shade or with a quick rinse, and our coastal influence keeps a lot of Ventura County yards milder than the inland deserts. We can also recommend heat-reducing turf products and infills for sun-exposed areas to keep it comfortable underfoot.

Is artificial turf good for dogs?

Yes — pet-rated turf is one of the most popular reasons homeowners make the switch. It drains quickly, won't get torn up or develop muddy spots, and rinses clean with a hose. With the right infill it resists odor, so pet areas stay fresh and easy to maintain.

How do I maintain artificial turf?

Very little is required. Rinse it occasionally, pick up debris and leaves, and give it a quick brush in high-traffic areas to keep the blades standing tall. There's no mowing, watering, fertilizing, or reseeding — which is exactly why so many people choose it over a living lawn.
