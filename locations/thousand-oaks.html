Local Roots

Landscaping for the Conejo Valley's Finest Homes

Thousand Oaks earned its name honestly — the city is dotted with the sprawling coast live oaks that residents fight to protect, and those mature trees set the tone for landscaping across the Conejo Valley. The housing here is substantial and well-established: ranch-style homes in Lynn Ranch and Wildwood, larger custom properties in North Ranch backing up to the golf courses, and newer luxury builds toward the Westlake Village line. Lots tend to be generous, expectations run high, and a meaningful share of neighborhoods sit inside HOAs with real standards for street-facing landscaping. Homeowners here aren't looking for the cheapest option — they want a yard that matches the home and holds its value.

Ventura Landscape & Design works across Thousand Oaks the way these properties deserve: thoughtful design, clean execution, and materials built to last. We're comfortable navigating North Ranch and Conejo HOA architectural-review processes, designing around protected oaks, and delivering the kind of outdoor-living spaces — built-in kitchens, fire features, refined hardscape — that Conejo Valley buyers expect. Whether you're upgrading a tired front yard for curb appeal or building out a resort-style backyard, we treat your home like the investment it is.

Local Questions

Thousand Oaks Landscaping FAQ

🌿
Do you handle North Ranch and HOA architectural review?

Yes. Many Thousand Oaks neighborhoods, especially North Ranch and the Conejo communities, require landscaping plans to clear an architectural review board. We prepare the design and documentation those boards expect so your project gets approved and moves forward smoothly.

Can you work around Thousand Oaks oak tree ordinances?

Absolutely. The city has strong protections for its native oaks, and permits or arborist sign-off can be required for work near them. We design hardscape and planting to respect protected root zones and keep your project compliant while preserving the trees that make the area special.

What does a backyard remodel cost in Thousand Oaks?

It depends heavily on scope — a refreshed planting design is very different from a full outdoor living build with kitchen, fire feature, and extensive hardscape. Lots here are larger, so we always start with a free, detailed estimate tailored to your property and goals.

Do you build outdoor kitchens and entertaining spaces?

We do, and they're popular in the Conejo Valley. Generous lots and a strong indoor-outdoor lifestyle make Thousand Oaks ideal for built-in BBQ islands, fire pits, covered patios, and full outdoor kitchens designed to match your home.

Can you make a large Conejo Valley yard more water-efficient?

Definitely. Bigger lots can mean bigger water bills, so we combine smart zoned irrigation, drought-tolerant planting, and artificial turf where it fits to cut usage substantially while keeping your landscape looking lush and upscale.
