Local Roots

Landscaping Built for Oxnard Living

Oxnard is the largest city in Ventura County, and it shows in the sheer variety of its yards. You've got brand-new developments like RiverPark and Seabridge with their tidy front yards and HOA rules, the spacious ranch-style lots out by the strawberry fields, the waterfront homes around Channel Islands Harbor, and older neighborhoods like La Colonia and downtown with mature trees and generous setbacks. The flat, fertile soil that built this town's agricultural reputation is the same soil sitting in most backyards — a real advantage when you know how to work it.

Ventura Landscape & Design works across all of Oxnard, and we plan around what each part of town actually needs. Near the harbor and the coast we lean on salt-tolerant plants and materials that handle the marine air; in the inland tracts we design water-wise yards that hold up through dry summers without running the bill sky-high. Whether it's a large family lot that needs a full backyard build or a commercial frontage that has to look sharp every day, we bring the crews and equipment to handle Oxnard at its scale.

Local Questions

Oxnard Landscaping FAQ

🌿
Do you serve all Oxnard neighborhoods?

Yes — RiverPark, Seabridge, the Channel Islands Harbor area, College Park, La Colonia, downtown, and the outlying ranch lots. We cover the whole city and the surrounding county.

How much does landscaping cost in Oxnard?

Oxnard's bigger lots mean a wider range than most towns. A front-yard refresh is one number; a full backyard with patio and turf on a large lot is another. We give free, itemized estimates so you know exactly what your project runs.

Do you handle HOA landscaping in Oxnard?

We do. Many of Oxnard's newer developments — RiverPark and Seabridge especially — have front-yard standards. We're familiar with what they require and design to pass review without the back-and-forth.

Can you landscape commercial properties in Oxnard?

Yes. We maintain and install for commercial frontages, business parks, and multi-unit properties across Oxnard, with reliable scheduled service to keep them looking sharp.

What grows well in Oxnard's soil and climate?

Oxnard sits on rich agricultural soil, which is great for lawns and most plantings. We mix in drought-tolerant species and smart irrigation to handle the dry summers, and salt-tolerant plants for properties closer to the harbor and coast.
