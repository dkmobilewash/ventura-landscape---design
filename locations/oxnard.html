Local Roots

Landscaping Built for Oxnard Living

Oxnard is the largest city in Ventura County, and the variety shows in its yards. There are waterfront homes around Channel Islands Harbor and Mandalay Bay with view lines and boat docks to design around, established neighborhoods like Rose Park with mature trees and generous lots, and brand-new master-planned tracts in RiverPark where the landscaping often starts as a blank slate. Wrapped around all of it are the strawberry fields and farmland that built this town — fertile soil, flat ground, and full sun. Many Oxnard properties sit on bigger lots than you'll find tucked into the coast in Ventura, which means there's real room to work with.

We work across every part of it. Whether you're a homeowner near the harbor who needs salt-tolerant plants and a wind-smart layout, a family in a new development starting from bare dirt, or a commercial property owner who needs a clean, low-maintenance look that holds up, Landscaping Pros Of Ventura brings the same crew and the same standards. We're just up the road, we know Oxnard's soils and water rules, and we treat a big Oxnard lot as an opportunity rather than a chore.

Local Questions

Oxnard Landscaping FAQ

🌿
Do you serve all Oxnard neighborhoods?

Yes — Channel Islands Harbor, Mandalay Bay, RiverPark, Rose Park, Oxnard Shores, College Park, and everything in between. We cover the whole city plus the surrounding farmland communities.

How much does landscaping cost in Oxnard?

Oxnard lots run larger than average, so cost depends a lot on square footage and scope. A new RiverPark backyard from bare dirt is a bigger project than refreshing a front yard. We give free, itemized estimates so you know exactly what your project runs.

Can you landscape a brand-new construction yard?

Definitely — a lot of our Oxnard work is new builds in RiverPark and other developments where the lot is just graded dirt. We handle design, irrigation, sod or turf, hardscaping, and planting so you start with a finished yard.

Do you work on commercial properties in Oxnard?

We do. We install and maintain landscaping for Oxnard commercial sites and HOAs, with low-maintenance, professional designs that keep the property looking sharp year-round.

What plants hold up near the Oxnard harbor?

Close to Channel Islands and Mandalay Bay you're dealing with salt air and steady wind. We lean on salt-tolerant natives, succulents, ornamental grasses, and hardy shrubs that won't get burned by the conditions.
