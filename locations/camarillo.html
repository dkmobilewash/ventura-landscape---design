Local Roots

Landscaping Built for Camarillo Living

Camarillo is a city of well-kept neighborhoods, and it's hard to miss. Master-planned communities like Mission Oaks, Village at the Park, and Spanish Hills set a high bar, with Spanish-style and Mediterranean homes whose red-tile roofs practically ask for clean, structured front yards. Many of these tracts sit under active HOAs with real guidelines about plant palettes, lawn condition, and what's visible from the street. Up on the hillsides you get views and slopes that need thoughtful grading and the right erosion-conscious planting. Camarillo also sees more sun and a little more heat than the coast, which shapes what thrives here.

That mix of pride of ownership and HOA expectations is right in our wheelhouse. Ventura Landscape & Design designs and installs front yards that sail through HOA approval, builds backyards worth showing off, and keeps everything maintained to the standard Camarillo neighborhoods are known for. We know the local plant palette, the heat, and the slope challenges, so your landscape looks sharp and stays that way.

Local Questions

Camarillo Landscaping FAQ

🌿
Do you serve all Camarillo neighborhoods?

Yes — Mission Oaks, Village at the Park, Spanish Hills, Las Posas Estates, Camarillo Heights, and the newer tracts throughout the city. We cover all of Camarillo and the surrounding area.

Do you handle HOA landscaping in Camarillo?

Constantly. Many Camarillo communities have HOA guidelines on plant choices, lawn condition, and front-yard appearance. We design to those rules and can help with submittals so your project gets approved the first time.

How much does landscaping cost in Camarillo?

It depends on the scope — an HOA-compliant front yard refresh runs very differently from a full hillside backyard with retaining walls and an outdoor kitchen. We provide free, detailed estimates specific to your Camarillo property.

Can you landscape a sloped or hillside yard?

Absolutely. Hillside homes around Spanish Hills and Camarillo Heights need proper grading, retaining walls, and erosion-conscious planting. We build hillside landscapes that are stable, usable, and good-looking.

What plants do well in Camarillo's climate?

Camarillo gets more sun and heat than the coast, so we favor Mediterranean plants, drought-tolerant natives, and water-wise selections that look great with Spanish-style architecture and hold up in the warmth.
