Local Roots

Landscaping Built for Ojai Living

Ojai is unlike anywhere else in the county — a small, artistic town tucked into a crescent valley that glows pink when the sun sets behind the Topatopa Mountains. Homes here run from Spanish-style and California ranch estates along the arcade and Grand Avenue to rustic East End properties shaded by centuries-old coast live oaks. The valley runs hot and dry inland, and many residents — drawn by Ojai's wellness and resort culture — want gardens that feel calm, natural, and rooted in the landscape: native oaks, olive trees, lavender, and meditative outdoor spaces rather than thirsty manicured lawns.

Landscaping Pros Of Ventura has worked the Ojai Valley long enough to respect what makes it special. We design around the existing oaks instead of fighting them, lean into drought-tolerant and California-native plant palettes that fit both the climate and the local aesthetic, and build outdoor rooms that frame those mountain views. Whether you're restoring a historic estate, softening a new build, or creating a quiet retreat garden, we deliver work that belongs in Ojai.

Local Questions

Ojai Landscaping FAQ

🌿
Can you landscape around protected oak trees?

Yes, and it's something we take seriously in Ojai. Coast live oaks are protected and easily damaged by grading, irrigation, or fill placed over their roots. We design within the dripline carefully, avoid summer watering near the trunk, and keep your oaks healthy while still creating the garden you want.

What plants fit the Ojai Valley aesthetic?

Native and Mediterranean species suit both the climate and the look — California natives, olive trees, lavender, rosemary, ornamental grasses, manzanita, and oak understory plantings. They handle the inland heat, sip water, and give you that natural, valley-rooted feel rather than a fussy formal garden.

Do you work on larger Ojai estates?

We do. From East End acreage to estate properties off Grand Avenue and McNell Road, we handle full-property design and installation — courtyards, retreat gardens, native restoration, hardscaping, and view-framing outdoor living spaces.

How do you handle Ojai's hot, dry summers?

The valley sees real inland heat once you're away from the coast. We combine drought-tolerant planting, mulch, and efficient drip irrigation on smart controllers so your landscape looks good through August without driving up your water bill.

Can you keep lighting dark-sky friendly?

Absolutely. Ojai values its dark night skies, so we use low, shielded, warm-toned fixtures that light paths and highlight trees without throwing glare upward. You get safety and ambiance while keeping those famous Ojai stars visible.
