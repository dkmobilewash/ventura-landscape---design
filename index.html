Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

🌿
What areas does Landscaping Pros Of Ventura serve?

We provide landscaping services throughout Ventura and the surrounding communities, including Oxnard, Channel Islands, Camarillo, Saticoy, and Santa Paula. As a local Ventura-based company, we know the area's climate and soil and tailor every project to it.

What landscaping services do you offer in Ventura, CA?

We offer full-service landscaping: landscape design and installation, ongoing landscape maintenance, lawn care, irrigation, tree services, artificial turf, hardscaping, retaining walls, paver patios, outdoor lighting, outdoor kitchens, and overall curb appeal and property enhancement. We handle both design and installation in-house.

Can you design a drought-tolerant or low-water landscape for my Ventura yard?

Yes. Southern California's dry climate and water-conservation rules make water-wise landscaping a smart investment. We design drought-tolerant landscapes and install artificial turf and efficient irrigation systems that cut water use while keeping your yard looking great year-round.

Do you install hardscaping such as paver patios, retaining walls, and outdoor kitchens?

Absolutely. Our hardscaping services include paver patios, retaining walls, outdoor kitchens, and outdoor lighting. These features add usable outdoor living space and boost your property's value and curb appeal across Ventura County.

Do you provide ongoing lawn care and landscape maintenance?

Yes. Beyond design and installation, we offer recurring landscape maintenance and lawn care to keep your property healthy and well-kept through every season. We serve homeowners and properties throughout Ventura, Oxnard, Camarillo, and the surrounding area.
