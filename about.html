A Local Team That Treats Your Yard Like Our Own

Ventura Landscape & Design is a full-service landscaping and outdoor living company built right here in Ventura County.

Our Story

Rooted in Ventura County

Ventura Landscape & Design started with a simple idea: homeowners deserve one local company that can handle the whole outdoor project — from the first design sketch to the finished patio, and the maintenance that keeps it beautiful for years.

Too many homeowners get bounced between a designer, a separate hardscape crew, an irrigation guy, and a mow-and-go service that doesn't talk to any of them. We do it all in-house, so the vision stays intact and there's one team accountable for the result.

We've spent years working in yards from the Ventura coast to the Conejo Valley. We know our soils, our salt air, our inland heat, and the water restrictions that shape responsible landscaping in Southern California. That local knowledge shows up in every plant we choose and every system we install.
